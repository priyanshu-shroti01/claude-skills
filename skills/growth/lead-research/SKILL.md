# Lead Research

Build a qualified lead list from legitimate public sources — the way you'd
be comfortable explaining to the lead. Run by lead-scout, only on explicit
human request. **The evaluated Scout tool is BLOCKED**
(ToS/GDPR exposure — docs/dependencies.md); this skill is the lawful
manual method.

## Hard limits (restating the charter — binding)

No spam · no automated/bulk contact · **no outreach at all** (human
approval gate, human sends or approves each campaign) · no auth/paywall/
rate-limit bypass · no platform-ToS violations (rules out scraping most
social platforms) · no sensitive personal data · no guessed/derived email
addresses, no SMTP probing.

## 1. Define the ICP (with product-director/growth-agent)

Industry/segment · size band · geography · trigger signals (hiring,
launching, outdated web presence, funding news) · disqualifiers · what
"qualified" means in one sentence.

## 2. Source sweep (allowlist)

Company websites (about/contact/press) · public business directories and
registries · published news/press releases · industry association member
lists (public) · search engines over the above. Per source: is this
information the business published for business contact? Unclear
legality/terms → skip and note it.

## 3. Qualify & record

Per candidate against the ICP; discard cheaply, record thoroughly:

```
COMPANY | WHY QUALIFIED (vs. ICP, specific) | SOURCE URLS |
PUBLIC BUSINESS CONTACT (only if published for that purpose) |
SUGGESTED ANGLE (unsent draft, grounded in their public situation) |
CONFIDENCE (high/med/low)
```

Deduplicate; date-stamp the list.

## 4. Handoff (approval gate)

Deliver the list + proposed next step to the human. Any outreach happens
outside this skill: human-approved, human-owned, compliant with
applicable law (flag GDPR/CAN-SPAM questions for the human's counsel —
don't lawyer them yourself).

## Storage

Lead lists live in the project's private working files — never in
claude-mem global memory, never committed to public repos.
