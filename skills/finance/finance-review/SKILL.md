# Finance Review

Finance work here prepares a decision; it does not make it. Show the figures,
show the options and their effect, and let the owner choose. Never answer a
spending question with a bare yes or no.

## The three numbers, always together

For the period in question (default: this month so far, and last full month):
- **Sales**: money actually received, not invoiced. Say which.
- **Expenses**: recorded spending, by category, with recurring costs separated
  from one-off costs.
- **Profit**: sales minus expenses, and the cash position if known.

State the source and the as-of time of each figure. A number without a source
is not used.

## Expense analysis

1. List recurring costs with their renewal dates and what they are for.
2. Flag: costs with no clear owner or use, duplicates (two tools doing one
   job), costs that grew faster than sales, and annual renewals in the next
   60 days.
3. For each flagged cost, give the saving and the consequence of cutting it.
   Never recommend cutting something whose purpose you do not know; ask.

## Budget tracking

- Budget vs actual per category, with the variance in amount and percent.
- Explain a variance only from a recorded transaction, never by assumption.
- Projection to period end: say how it was computed (run rate, known renewals).

## Invoice review

- Check: the payer, the amount against the agreed price, the tax, the due
  date, duplicates, and that a payment marked received matches a transaction.
- Overdue: list by age (0-30, 31-60, 60+ days) with the amount and the last
  contact. A reminder is customer-visible and needs the owner's approval.
- Never mark an invoice paid, issue a credit or a refund: those move money
  and are the owner's decision through the billing system.

## "Can we afford it?"

Answer with:
1. The cost (one-off and recurring), and when it would be paid.
2. This month's sales, expenses and profit with and without it.
3. What it displaces or risks (cash buffer, a renewal, a target).
4. Options: now, later (when), cheaper alternative, not at all.
Then stop. The owner decides.

## Hard rules

- Currency as recorded; never convert without saying the rate and its date.
- Do not round away small amounts that add up; totals must reconcile.
- No financial commitment, purchase, payment or contract on anyone's behalf.
- Personal and business money are reported separately unless told otherwise.
