# Workflow: Premium Website

**Trigger**: flagship sites where design distinction is the point —
brand experiences, product showcases, portfolio-grade work.
**Orchestrator**: commander. Extends `new-website`; only the differences
are listed here.

## Amplified stages

- **Research+** — research packet must include 2–3 *contrasting* visual
  directions with named references; competitor matrix mandatory; the
  human picks/blends the direction before design begins.
- **Design language+** — DESIGN.md gets a signature: one ownable visual
  device (typographic system, interaction motif, illustration language,
  spatial concept) named and specified. No signature → not premium, back
  to direction work.
- **Motion as a first-class track** — motion-director designs the motion
  language alongside visual design, not after: page transitions, scroll
  narrative, micro-interactions, with performance budgets attached.
- **Media & 3D tracks (as routed)** — media-director runs full
  image/video pipelines for hero-grade assets; 3d-director /
  exploded-view-director build routed 3D experiences (r3f-scenes budgets,
  fallbacks mandatory).
- **Anti-slop, strict mode** — the gate runs at stage boundaries, not
  once at the end: after visual design (on rendered comps) and after
  build. The meta-test (logo-swap) must fail *convincingly* — a premium
  site is unmistakably its brand's.
- **Performance under beauty** — budgets are NOT relaxed: the craft is
  hitting LCP ≤2.5s *with* the rich media. performance-agent reviews
  asset/scene plans before they're built, not just after.

## Extra gates

- Design-director sign-off on rendered pages per template (not per site).
- Cross-browser visual QA (Chromium/Firefox/WebKit) mandatory.
- Human walkthrough of the staged site before L5 — premium work ships
  when the human says it feels right, not when the checklist alone passes.

## Rules

Everything from `new-website` still applies — premium adds ambition,
never subtracts discipline.
